# Usability test: Guided and Pro (UI plan, Phase 7)

A short, moderated test to check that a beginner can design and publish a tee
unaided, and that an experienced designer isn't slowed down. Run it alongside
Anastasia's interview (`Questions Anastasia.docx`): her answers on 2D freedom,
real-time 3D, ease against freedom, and devices decide what Guided shows by
default.

## Who

- **Beginners (3 to 5):** have never used a garment design tool. Start them in
  Guided (the default), on a laptop.
- **Experienced designers (2 to 3), Anastasia included:** use Illustrator, CLO,
  Procreate or similar. Let them switch Pro tools on whenever they look for it,
  and note when and why they do. If they bring an iPad and Pencil, run the same
  tasks on it.

## Setup

- A fresh account per person, on staging or the local build.
- Screen and voice recording, with consent.
- No help or demo beforehand. The moderator only answers "What do you expect
  to happen?" or "Where would you look?"

## Tasks

Read each task aloud and start a timer. Stop at success, at a give-up, or after
four minutes.

| # | Task | Success |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | "Make a new T-shirt design." | Reaches the Design step |
| 2 | "Make the shirt a dark colour." | Base colour changed on Color & Pattern |
| 3 | "Put your name on the back." | A word on the Back panel |
| 4 | "Add a graphic to the front." | A graphic on the Front panel |
| 5 | "Draw something on one sleeve." | Ink on a sleeve panel |
| 6 | "Make the graphic bigger and turn it slightly." | Scale and rotation changed |
| 7 | "Go back to how it looked before your last change." | Undo, or a restore from History |
| 8 | "Share it so a friend can see it." | Published, and the link copied |

## Record for each task

- Completed (yes, with a hint, or no), and the time taken.
- The first place they looked, and every wrong turn.
- Words they used for things. Mismatches with our labels feed the copy (for
  example "flip" for Mirror to other side, or "left arm" for Left sleeve).
- For experienced designers: what they missed in Guided, and whether Pro had it.

## Questions afterwards

1. "What was hardest?"
2. "Was the 3D tee useful while you worked, or did you mostly look at the flat panels?" (interview Q5 to Q7)
3. "Did anything feel like it was hiding from you?" (Guided against Pro, interview Q9)
4. "Would you use this on an iPad with a pencil?" (interview Q11)

## What changes as a result

- A task most beginners fail in Guided means a default or a label is wrong.
  Fix it before adding features.
- A control most experienced designers reach for in Guided moves out of Pro.
- Record the results and decisions in `docs/decisions.md`, with the date.
